
# Analysis of Kia Vehicle Thefts

## 🚘 Overview
This project analyzes data related to the rise in thefts of Kia vehicles, especially after viral social media trends exposed ignition vulnerabilities. The goal is to identify patterns in theft incidents and understand how factors like location, time, and vehicle models contribute to this phenomenon.

## 📊 Objectives
- Identify geographic hotspots for Kia thefts
- Examine time-based trends (e.g., monthly/yearly spikes)
- Analyze which models and years are most frequently targeted

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📁 Dataset
- **Source:** Public police reports or aggregated auto theft datasets
- **Columns:** Date, City, State, Car Make, Car Model, Year, Recovery Status, etc.

## 📈 Key Findings
- Thefts spiked significantly post-2021 in urban areas
- Older Kia models without immobilizers are disproportionately targeted
- Theft hotspots include regions with higher population density

## 🔄 Future Enhancements
