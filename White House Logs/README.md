# White House Visitor Logs Analysis

## 🎯 Overview
This project analyzes the White House visitor logs, also known as the White House Worker and Visitor Entry System (WAVES). These logs record individuals visiting the White House to meet with the President or other officials. The dataset offers insights into the frequency, timing, and nature of visits, shedding light on the transparency and accessibility of the U.S. executive branch.

## 🧠 Objectives
- Analyze visitor trends by date, department, and purpose.
- Identify patterns in visitor frequency and scheduling.
- Assess the completeness and transparency of the records.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib, Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📁 Dataset
- **Source:** [White House Visitor Records](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/briefing-room/disclosures/visitor-records)
- **Features:** Appointment number, badge number, access type, time of arrival and departure, appointment dates, visitor names, meeting location, and caller information.

## 🔍 Key Findings
- **Visitor Frequency:** Identified peak periods of visitor activity.
- **Departmental Analysis:** Analyzed visitor data by department or office.
- **Transparency Assessment:** Evaluated the completeness of the logs, noting any missing or redacted information.

## 📈 Visualizations
The project includes various visualizations to illustrate the findings:
- **Time Series:** Tracking visitor counts over time.
- **Bar Charts:** Displaying visitor counts by department or purpose.
- **Heatmaps:** Showing patterns in visitor scheduling.
